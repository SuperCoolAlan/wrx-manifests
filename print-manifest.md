### Discrepancy — RESOLVED 2026-08-19
- Spreadsheet says **"APS 525 FMIC"** ($300, from Bryce Skelton); **Alan confirms the installed unit is a 725.**
- Not a typo: APS built a whole **D/R Series** — D/R 500, 525, 650, 725. The number is the hp rating. **D/R 525 = 350-525 hp; D/R 725 = 500-725 hp.** At a 500whp target the 525 would be marginal, so the distinction matters.
- Either the sheet entry is mislabeled or a 525 preceded the 725. **Verify by measuring the core: the 725 is 28" x 9.5" x 4.5"** per `aps_dr725_kit_inventory.md`.
- "D/R" is APS's series prefix; what it stands for is **not documented** in any surviving source (APS is defunct, site dead). Treat as an opaque model code.

## 2011 Manual — Section Map Caveat

The 2011 PDF (3,350 pp) has **no bookmarks**. A text scan of page codes produced 958 transitions, meaning sections are heavily interleaved and the auto-map is **noisy and not trustworthy** for print ranges. First-occurrence pages for the sections you care about:

| DS | 106 | Drive Shaft |
| DI | 233 | Differentials |
| CS | 1531 | **Control Systems (DCCD)** |

**Recommendation:** use the **2007 modular PDFs** as the readable working documents for 6MT/DCCD (they're clean, standalone, and the 6MT is the same DCCD family), and dig into the 2011 monolith only to verify specific torque values and fluid specs. Verify each page range visually before printing.
