# APS DR725 FMIC Kit — Expected Inventory & Verification

## Kit identification

- **Manufacturer:** Air Power Systems (APS Performance), Australia — defunct, original site (airpowersystems.com) is dead
- **Model:** D/R Series 725 Front Mount Intercooler — "DR725"
- **Variants:**
  - `WRX-FMI/R4` — polished/silver piping
  - `WRX-FMI/R4B` — black piping (some kits "crinkle red")
- **Application:** 2002-2005 WRX/STI primary; 2006-2007 fitment requires extra/different parts
- **Power rating:** 500-725 hp (50-75 lb/min turbo)
- **Core:** 28" × 9.5" × 4.5" bar-and-plate, ~266 sq in face, ~1,197 cu in volume

## Year-specific notes

The kit shipped with **different parts for 02-03 vs 04-05 vs 06-07**. Things that change year to year:
- Bumper beam bracket geometry (crash structure differs)
- Crossover hose length through the fenderwell
- Some bracket / hardware kits are year-specific

User chassis = **2004 WRX** → standard 04-05 install kit applies.

## Known issues / things that often need addressing

### Fitment quirks documented in forums

- **Timing belt cover clearance** — turbo-side piping can rub the timing belt cover. Some installers grind a small relief or use slight routing tweak. Watch for this on install.
- **Hood scoop / cold air intake conflict** — the hot-side charge pipe routes through the area where the OEM TMIC sat, which is also where stock-style cold air intakes route. **This is your known issue** (per build notes).
- **Crank pulley / lower radiator hose clearance** — cold-side piping can be tight here on some chassis.
- **Stainless steel piping is heavy and rigid** — install requires patience, dry-fit everything before final tightening, otherwise you'll fight alignment.

### Commonly missing or worn-out parts on used kits

When inventorying a used kit, these are the most-commonly-lost or replaced items:

- ⚠️ **Silicone couplers** — can dry out, crack at clamp marks, or get cut up during removal
- ⚠️ **T-bolt clamps** — sometimes seller keeps "the good ones" for their next build
- ⚠️ **Coolant overflow bottle bracket** — easily lost during disassembly
- ⚠️ **BOV vacuum line + fittings** — often missing
- ⚠️ **Original BOV** — sometimes swapped for a different brand / sold separately
- ⚠️ **Mounting hardware bag** — bolts/washers commonly lost
- ⚠️ **Year-specific bracket pieces** if kit was originally for a different year

## Replacement source plan (for missing parts)

### Silicone couplers

Standard sizes can be sourced separately from:
- **Vibrant Performance** — common quality silicone couplers, available at Summit, JEGS, Amazon
- **Mishimoto** — also makes individual couplers
- **Siliconehoses.com** — wide selection, custom sizes
- **eBay** — generic 4-ply silicone couplers ~$10-25 each (acceptable for non-critical positions; verify wall thickness)

For a **5-7 coupler restock** plus T-bolt clamps, budget **$80-200** depending on quality tier.

### T-bolt clamps

- **Norma / Mikalor** — German-made T-bolts, ~$5-8 each, the gold standard
- **Vibrant** — solid mid-tier, ~$4-6 each
- **eBay generic** — $1-3 each, fine for non-pressure-critical positions
- Buy 2-4 spares — they're consumables and you'll need them eventually

### BOV / BPV

If the Turbosmart Raceport is missing:
- **Turbosmart Raceport new** — ~$280-330
- **Turbosmart Kompact Plumb Back** (recirc version) — ~$280-330 — better for the FMIC + Link standalone tune
- **Tial Q 50mm** — ~$280-350, popular alternative
- **GFB Respons / TMS** — ~$200-280 mid-tier options

### Coolant overflow bottle

- **OEM Subaru coolant bottle** — fits but mounting may differ
- **Aftermarket round overflow tank** (Spectre, Mishimoto, Aeromotive) — ~$30-80
- 3D-printed mount can fill the gap if the original bracket is missing

### Hardware

- **OEM Subaru bumper beam hardware** — fits, available from any dealer
- **Generic 8.8 metric bolts** for non-structural mounts — hardware store

## Action items

1. **Spread the kit out and photograph it** — multiple angles, every component
2. **Count the silicone couplers** — note size (use calipers if you have them) and shape (straight, reducer, elbow)
3. **Compare to the inventory checklist above** — mark what's present, what's missing
4. **Check coupler condition** — flexion, no cracks at clamp marks, no oil saturation
5. **Identify the year-variant** — does the bumper bracket match 04-05 WRX geometry?
6. **Decide:** restock missing silicones + clamps as a single $80-200 order, or just buy a complete set new from Vibrant/Mishimoto for a clean baseline
7. **Bench-fit before engine install** — dry-fit the whole charge path on the bumper before committing to engine assembly order

## Open questions to resolve before install

- [ ] Does your APS 65mm CAI fit alongside the FMIC piping? (Per earlier notes — known conflict; may need to route differently or replace)
- [ ] Is the coolant overflow bottle present + bracket usable?
- [ ] Is the original bumper-beam bracket structurally sound (no cracks, bent mounts)?
- [ ] BOV — is it still the Turbosmart Raceport, or has it been swapped?
